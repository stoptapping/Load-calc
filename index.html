    AlphaCore Solutions LLC · USDOT 5414341 · MC-90307121

    Diesel default: AAA Texas average, $5.689/gal on Oct 9, 2026 (Houston $5.742, McAllen $5.313, Brownsville $5.389). Check today's Texas price.

    Costs counted: only what each trip spends (fuel, DEF, idle fuel, tolls and permits, other trip costs, road costs per night, driver pay, and per-load fees). Monthly bills, maintenance and tires are left out.

    Hours of service: FMCSA 49 CFR 395 (11 h driving, 14 h window, 10 h off, 30-min break after 8 h driving). Texas intrastate: 12 h driving, 15 h on duty, 8 h off.

    MPG and DEF defaults are estimates. Replace them with your own numbers. Not modeled: the 60/70-hour weekly limit, traffic, weather, and broker payment terms.
